The world through their eyes

What can surviving maps, written accounts and research actually tell us about how people understood the world around them? Follow the evidence — and see where it stops.

Not a reconstructed view of what every person knew. Maps made by particular people, dated objects, surviving descriptions and carefully qualified links.
