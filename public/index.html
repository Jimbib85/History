ABOUT THIS LITTLE EXPERIMENT

A history atlas for curious minds.

This is a personal passion project. Jump between civilisations, see who shared the same centuries, and peek at how historical mapmakers imagined the world.

Map honesty: markers represent approximate places associated with societies, not their territorial borders. Historical maps are authentic surviving examples or clearly labelled later copies and reconstructions. There is no pretence that a single map represents everybody's knowledge.

Dates: ancient and medieval dates are often approximate. The atlas currently contains a small hand-curated starter collection intended to grow over time.

Tip: press / to search, click a civilisation, or hit Surprise me to go somewhere unexpected.
