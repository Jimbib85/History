# World History Atlas - 1501-2026 CE

**Priority historical research collection | Research cut-off: 22 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane**

## Executive assessment

This collection prioritises transformations in political institutions, coerced and voluntary mobility, food systems, scientific knowledge, industry, public health, communications and the environment. It does not rank societies on an "advanced versus primitive" scale or equate military victory with lasting historical influence. Regional importance, affected communities and durable institutional consequences shape selection.

The inclusive requested window is **1501-2026 CE**, represented by `[1501, 2027)`: **526 numbered years**, with 2026 only observed through **22 October 2026**. It is an authorised macro-period, not a 500-year pack. Current assessments are kept separate from the year they measure: a publication released in 2026 can report 2025 observations.

This release contains 77 selected event/process/observation records and 34 technology observations. It rechecks and re-expresses priority material rather than republishing every row of the older modern-period catalogue as verified. It is not a complete history of every country or a complete 2026 news chronology.

## 1. 1501-1700: several powerful regional systems, increasingly connected

The early modern story is not a simple handover from passive Asian and African societies to Europe. Safavid state formation from 1501, Ottoman expansion into Mamluk Egypt in 1517, Mughal consolidation under Akbar, and Qing rule from 1644 represent major changes within powerful regional political systems. Court patronage, religious institutions, administration and urban development are historical changes in their own right, not merely background to European expansion. [src_safavid](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/the-art-of-the-safavids-before-1600) [src_ottoman](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/the-art-of-the-ottomans-before-1600) [src_mughal](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/the-art-of-the-mughals-before-1600) [src_qing](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/the-qing-dynasty-1644-1911-painting)

Tokugawa-period Japan supplies a useful contrast. The reviewed Edo art history describes urban markets, patronage and print culture within a regulated system of foreign contact. Its 1615-1868 artistic period is not used as the founding date of the shogunate. Restricted contact is also not translated into a false claim of total isolation. [src_edo](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/art-of-the-edo-period-1615-1868)

Conquest in the Americas must preserve Indigenous agency and continuity. The Tenochtitlan record separates the 1521 siege from a seventeenth-century painting used in a later historical representation. For the Andean encounters of 1532-1533, the inspected scholarly annotation emphasises a multi-actor interpretation rather than a simple two-person confrontation. It was not access to the full article, and its limits remain on the source record. Political defeat is not coded as disappearance of Mexica, Inca or other Indigenous communities. [src_tenoch](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/exploring-the-early-americas/interactives/conquest-of-mexico-paintings/painting7/detail2.html) [src_cajamarca](https://www.loc.gov/item/hlas-bi2022001168/)

Potosi links colonial extraction, water-powered infrastructure and labour organisation. Hydraulic mills and managed water were part of an influential silver-producing complex; coercive labour was a social and political arrangement, not a technical achievement. The 1570-1600 record is an explicit observation window, not a claim that mining or coercion began and ended with those dates. [src_potosi](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/420/)

The English East India Company's formation in 1600 illustrates how a commercial institution could become a major political actor over time. The founding record does not backdate all its later territorial power. The separate 1857-1858 observations concern uprising and the transfer from Company to Crown rule, leaving room for different participants, local objectives and contested names for the rebellion. [src_eic](https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/why-did-indian-mutiny-happen) [src_1857](https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/decisive-events-indian-mutiny)

## 2. Forced migration, emancipation and unequal claims to rights

SlaveVoyages reconstructs approximately **12.52 million embarkations** of enslaved Africans in its 1501-1866 trans-Atlantic coverage. The measure is neither an exact census nor a count of everyone who died because of slavery. It also does not cover all forms of slavery, all African forced migration or all Indian Ocean traffic. Treating it as an attributed statistical reconstruction is more informative than presenting a false exact global total. [src_slave_est](https://www.slavevoyages.org/assessment/estimates)

Rights history requires the same care about scope. The American declaration of 1776 and French declaration of 1789 are records of political claims and institutional change, not evidence that every person immediately possessed equal rights. The Haitian Revolution of 1791-1804 foregrounds a different process: enslaved people's struggle, emancipation and independence. Brazil's separation from Portugal is recorded as a process with regional disputes, not one universally accepted speech or date. [src_us1776](https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/declaration-of-independence) [src_fr1789](https://www.elysee.fr/en/french-presidency/the-declaration-of-the-rights-of-man-and-of-the-citizen) [src_haiti](https://history.state.gov/milestones/1784-1800/haitian-rev) [src_brazil](https://pesquisaescolar.fundaj.gov.br/pt-br/artigo/independencia-ou-morte-processo-historico-de-separacao-entre-brasil-e-portugal/)

The British Caribbean sequence distinguishes the prohibition of the slave trade from emancipation and the subsequent coercive apprenticeship regime. The relevant act dates to 1833, implementation to 1834, and the end of apprenticeship to 1838. In the United States, the Thirteenth Amendment was ratified in December 1865; its punishment-for-crime exception is preserved in the summary. Neither episode is represented as the end of all exploitation or a worldwide abolition date. [src_british_abolition](https://historicengland.org.uk/research/inclusive-heritage/the-slave-trade-and-abolition/sites-of-memory/ending-slavery/slavery-after-1807/) [src_us_abolition](https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/13th-amendment)

Australia and Aotearoa are not introduced as empty destinations. The New South Wales penal colony from 1788 is paired with Pemulwuy's resistance to colonial expansion. The Treaty of Waitangi is recorded with its initial February 1840 signing and the distinction between language texts, rather than a frictionless transfer of universally agreed sovereignty. The New Zealand suffrage milestone of 1893 concerns parliamentary voting rights; it is not expanded into a claim of complete gender or political equality. [src_nsw](https://www.nma.gov.au/defining-moments/resources/first-bigge-report) [src_pemulwuy](https://www.nma.gov.au/defining-moments/resources/pemulwuy) [src_waitangi](https://nzhistory.govt.nz/the-treaty-of-waitangi-is-signed) [src_waitangi_text](https://www.waitangitribunal.govt.nz/en/about/the-treaty/about-the-treaty) [src_suffrage](https://nzhistory.govt.nz/politics/womens-suffrage)

## 3. Print, knowledge and scientific explanation

Luther's 1517 printed critique provides evidence for circulation of religious argument. The surviving printed edition does not independently authenticate every familiar story about the posting of the theses. Copernicus's 1543 publication is likewise a milestone in the circulation of a heliocentric system, not an instant, universally accepted end to earlier astronomy. [src_luther](https://www.loc.gov/item/2021667736/) [src_copernicus](https://www.loc.gov/item/92516339/)

Newton's 1687 laws-of-motion text illustrates mathematical formalisation; only the cited extract and associated publication information were inspected, not every part of the Principia. In the nineteenth century, the joint communication of Darwin and Wallace in 1858 and Darwin's subsequent book in 1859 separate independent work, correspondence, institutional presentation and publication. A history of ideas is more accurate when it retains these collaborative and cumulative processes. [src_newton](https://newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/NATP00076) [src_darwin_wallace](https://www.linnean.org/the-society/history-of-science/alfred-russel-wallace) [src_darwin_origin](https://darwin-online.org.uk/content/contentblock?basepage=1&hitpage=520&itemID=F387&viewtype=text)

The collection's scientific entries are selective. It does not yet provide an adequate history of non-European mathematics, medical traditions, astronomical observation or the many people who supplied specimens, measurements and local knowledge. Those omissions are research priorities rather than proof that influential science belonged to a small set of famous European individuals.

## 4. Industry and infrastructure: invention is only one step

Watt's separate-condenser patent in 1769 is an improvement to an existing technological field, not the invention of all steam power. The 1825 Stockton and Darlington demonstration is a different kind of milestone: steam traction within a public railway system. The museum explicitly distinguishes Locomotion from the first steam locomotive and notes that regular operation continued to include horse haulage. A date labelled simply "railways invented" would lose the important part of this history. [src_watt](https://blog.sciencemuseum.org.uk/james-watt-and-the-separate-condenser/) [src_rail1825](https://blog.railwaymuseum.org.uk/shildon-the-cradle-of-the-railways-and-locomotion-no-1/)

Faraday's 1831 ring-coil experiment concerns electromagnetic induction. Pearl Street's 1882 electricity service concerns a working supply system with generators, distribution and users. They belong to a connected technological history, but a laboratory principle and an operating urban service are different record types. The Pearl Street catalogue object is a later model, so its creation date is not mistaken for the station's opening. [src_faraday](https://www.rigb.org/explore-science/explore/collection/michael-faradays-ring-coil-apparatus) [src_pearl](https://www.si.edu/object/nmah_712587)

The 1844 Washington-Baltimore telegraph demonstration is similarly scoped: a documented US milestone, not the first electric telegraph anywhere. Japanese industrial heritage between the 1850s and 1910 records transfer and adaptation across iron and steel, shipbuilding and coal mining. It is not modelled as passive copying or an automatic result of a single imperial decree. [src_telegraph](https://www.loc.gov/item/mcc00054/) [src_steel_japan](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1484/)

Industrial ammonia production at Oppau in 1913 separates laboratory chemistry from engineering at scale. Its agricultural and environmental consequences require additional independent quantitative research beyond a corporate chronology. The included record supports the industrial milestone, not a precise claim about how many people it fed or how much pollution it caused. [src_haber](https://www.basf.com/hr/hr/who-we-are/History/chronology/1902-1924/1913)

Industrial capacity did not eliminate political agency or guarantee a colonial outcome. The Berlin Conference of 1884-1885 concerned rules and claims; it did not draw every later African border at one meeting. Ethiopia's victory at Adwa in 1896 is a separate example of resistance with lasting regional importance. African political histories remain much broader than the colonial episodes selected here. [src_berlin](https://www.bpb.de/shop/zeitschriften/apuz/202989/bismarck-und-der-kolonialismus/) [src_adwa](https://www.si.edu/object/nmnhanthropology_8378154)

## 5. War, revolution, genocide and institutional rebuilding

The First World War is recorded with imperial mobilisation and affected communities rather than only European battlefields. Russia's revolutions in 1917 remain a sequence, with the calendar issue noted. The influenza pandemic of 1918-1919 is separate from the war, though the histories overlap. The Great Depression record makes its US institutional chronology explicit instead of imposing one recovery date on every region. [src_wwi](https://www.awm.gov.au/articles/atwar/first-world-war) [src_russia1917](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/education/students/videos/spotlight-on/spotlight-on-russian-revolution/) [src_flu](https://archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/flu/pandemic-resources/1918-commemoration/1918-pandemic-history.htm) [src_depression](https://www.federalreservehistory.org/essays/great-depression)

The Second World War entry uses the familiar 1939-1945 umbrella but explicitly warns that major warfare in Asia began earlier. The Holocaust is represented as persecution and genocide, not a battle with symmetrical "sides". The source-based figure of six million murdered European Jews is not added to an overlapping general war total or used as a count of all victims of Nazi persecution. [src_wwii](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/world-war-ii-in-depth) [src_holocaust](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/documenting-numbers-of-victims-of-the-holocaust-and-nazi-persecution) [src_holocaust_dates](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/introduction-to-the-holocaust)

The post-war period combined new international institutions with continuing coercion. The United Nations Charter entered into force in 1945, and the Universal Declaration of Human Rights was proclaimed in 1948. These were consequential institutional and normative developments, not proof that rights were already enforced everywhere. Bikini's nuclear testing and displacement illustrate material and human costs within the same broad post-war world. The numerical inconsistencies in the UNESCO summary are explicitly flagged rather than copied into the dataset. [src_un1945](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/history-of-the-un) [src_udhr](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights/) [src_bikini](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1339/)

## 6. Decolonisation, state formation and regional agency

Indonesia's August 1945 proclamation, the independence and partition of India and Pakistan in 1947, and the proclamation of the People's Republic of China in 1949 are distinct transformations. Their historical trajectories cannot be reduced to one Cold War alignment or an orderly replacement of colonial rule. The pack distinguishes proclamation from completion of a sovereignty struggle and partition from a purely constitutional change. [src_indonesia](https://anri.go.id/page/pameran-arsip-virtual-hut-75) [src_partition](https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/independence-and-partition-1947) [src_china1949](https://history.state.gov/milestones/1945-1952/chinese-rev)

Bandung in 1955 foregrounds Afro-Asian political initiative. The UN's 1960 declaration on colonial independence represents an institutional milestone within a much larger field of independence movements. The end of the Soviet Union in 1991 and South Africa's 1994 democratic transition add later transformations, but neither provides a complete regional social history. [src_bandung](https://history.state.gov/milestones/1953-1960/bandung-conf) [src_decolonisation](https://www.un.org/en/global-issues/decolonization/) [src_soviet_end](https://history.state.gov/milestones/1989-1992/collapse-soviet-union) [src_sa1994](https://mandates.un.org/mandate/A/RES/48/258)

Coverage is still thin on specific African states, Spanish American independence, the Korean and Vietnamese wars, the Middle East, Central Asia, Latin American dictatorships and democratic transitions. These are substantial omissions, not minor footnotes. They are listed as research gaps so the Atlas cannot mistake a selective catalogue for an exhaustive account.

## 7. Food, health and human capability

The 1796 vaccination work is kept separate from older variolation practices and the much later campaign that eradicated smallpox. Penicillin likewise has separate discovery and therapeutic/manufacturing milestones: the 1928 observation did not immediately make a mass-produced medicine available. These distinctions connect technical knowledge to institutions, trials, production and access. [src_smallpox](https://www.who.int/news-room/spotlight/history-of-vaccination/history-of-smallpox-vaccination) [src_penicillin](https://www.acs.org/education/whatischemistry/landmarks/flemingpenicillin.html)

IR8's 1966 rice release is represented as crop improvement and institutional collaboration, not rice domestication. A documented transfer to Colombian collaborators provides one actual diffusion link. It does not justify drawing a universal Green Revolution route or asserting identical outcomes for all farmers. The distribution of benefits, input requirements, land and water consequences remain areas for further research. [src_ir8](https://ricetoday.irri.org/in-retrospect-irri-annual-report-1966/) [src_ir8_transfer](https://news.irri.org/2016/11/ciat-celebrates-50th-anniversary-of-ir8.html)

Smallpox eradication in 1980 is not equivalent to the ending of the COVID-19 international emergency designation in 2023. COVID-19 was characterised as a pandemic in March 2020; ending the emergency designation did not mean that the virus disappeared or that all effects ended. The record structure preserves that distinction instead of using the last newsworthy date as a disease endpoint. [src_smallpox](https://www.who.int/news-room/spotlight/history-of-vaccination/history-of-smallpox-vaccination) [src_covid](https://www.who.int/europe/emergencies/situations/COVID-19)

## 8. Electronics, networks and biological information

A working point-contact transistor in 1947, monolithic integrated-circuit developments around 1959-1960, ARPANET in 1969, and the 1983 TCP/IP transition belong to related but distinct technical histories. The Web was proposed at CERN in 1989, and CERN's 1993 open release concerns software reuse and diffusion. **The Web is not the Internet**, and none of these milestones is the invention of every kind of computing. [src_transistor](https://www.computerhistory.org/siliconengine/invention-of-the-point-contact-transistor/) [src_ic](https://www.computerhistory.org/siliconengine/practical-monolithic-integrated-circuit-concept-patented/) [src_internet](https://www.internetsociety.org/internet/history-internet/brief-history-internet/) [src_web](https://home.cern/science/computing/the-birth-of-the-web)

The Human Genome Project's 1990-2003 programme produced a reference and collaborative infrastructure, not a perfectly complete sequence of every human genome. Programmable Cas9 research in 2012 and modified-nucleoside RNA research in 2005 are separately dated scientific observations. The later mRNA vaccine deployments required a wider chain of work; the dataset does not credit one experiment with the entire platform. [src_genome](https://www.genome.gov/about-genomics/educational-resources/fact-sheets/human-genome-project) [src_crispr](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1225829) [src_mrna2005](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/2023/weissman/facts/) [src_mrna](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/2023/advanced-information/)

The 2017 Transformer paper is included as an influential machine-learning architecture, not the beginning of artificial intelligence. The source access was abstract-level, so this pack does not claim to have audited every implementation or performance result. Apollo 11's 1969 landing represents another large collaborative technical system; the landing date and first-step timezone issue are distinguished in the notes. [src_transformer](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1706.03762) [src_apollo](https://www.nasa.gov/history/july-20-1969-one-giant-leap-for-mankind/)

## 9. Environment and the contemporary observation boundary

The Paris Agreement's 2015 adoption is separated from its later entry into force. The IPCC's 2023 synthesis assesses human-caused warming; the assessment date is not a proposed beginning of climate change. These are treaty and assessment records, not interchangeable measurements. [src_paris](https://unfccc.int/about-us/unfccc-archives/the-unfccc-archival-exhibition/the-paris-agreement-exhibit) [src_ipcc](https://report.ipcc.ch/ar6syr/headline.html)

The final observations deliberately retain their units and measurement years. ITU estimated around **six billion Internet users, or 74% of the world population, in 2025**. IRENA's April 2026 release reports **692 GW** of renewable electricity capacity added in 2025 and **5,149 GW** installed at year-end. These are capacity figures, not electricity generation or share of all energy. WMO assessed 2025 at approximately **1.43 degrees C above 1850-1900**, with its rank varying between datasets. An annual anomaly is not the Paris Agreement's long-term warming threshold. [src_itu2025](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/stat/default.aspx/) [src_irena2026](https://www.irena.org/News/pressreleases/2026/Apr/Near-700-GW-Surge-in-2025-Proves-Renewable-Energy-Resilience-DE) [src_wmo2025](https://wmo.int/publication-series/state-of-global-climate/state-of-global-climate-2025)

The package therefore reaches the requested 2026 research cut-off through dated observations and publications, but it does not pretend to contain a completed 2026 global chronology. It supplies no unverified current-conflict death totals, forecast events presented as completed history, or year-end 2026 figures.

## 10. Historical worldviews and "meanwhile elsewhere"

The map catalogue contains one carefully labelled case: a Mexico City map catalogued with "Tenochtitlan, 1521" in its title but described as produced about **1550**. The catalogue questions a traditional authorship attribution and identifies Nahuatl place-name signs. This is useful evidence about a particular map and its makers, not a complete reconstruction of what every member of an empire knew. The image itself was not inspected or redistributed. [src_mexico_map](https://www.loc.gov/resource/gdcwdl.wdl_00503/)

Ten comparison cards link underlying records. Examples contrast Mughal patronage with Potosi's labour and extraction systems; scientific publication with Edo print markets; international institution-building with nuclear testing; and seeds with packet-networking infrastructures. They are explanatory comparisons, not automatically evidence of contact or causation.

Only **one public geographical anchor**, the UNESCO property point for Potosi, is exported. No imperial boundaries, battlefield lines, shipping corridors or technology-diffusion arrows have been invented. This sparse geography is an explicit research limitation rather than false cartographic completeness. [src_potosi](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/420/)

**Source-access accounting:** 55 search extract; 17 partial web text; 1 catalogue annotation; 2 abstract. Sources counted here were consulted at the stated level; search extracts are not opened full documents. No claim is marked independently corroborated after a complete primary-source audit. Confidence describes an editorial assessment of the underlying assertion, not a calibrated probability or a substitute for evidence.

Claims are explicit summary assertions with source locators. They are not a fully atomic verification of every associated name, date bound or editorial interpretation. Identity-reference rows borrow their support from linked observations and must not be treated as complete biographies or polity profiles.

The software checks cover JSON Schema, IDs, foreign keys, dates, parity between formats, public-point geometry, repeated staging merges, hashes and ZIP integrity. Their executed results are in [quality_report.json](data/quality_report.json) and the release validation file. Software validity does not establish historical accuracy. **Full live-application regression and a production import were not run.**
