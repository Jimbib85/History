# World History Atlas: 1000–1499 CE research pack

Source: `World_History_Atlas_1000-1499_CE_Research_Pack(1).zip`, uploaded by the atlas owner on 21 October 2026. The combined `atlas-research-pack.json` reproduces all structured datasets in the supplied ZIP, including 723 claim-source links and the supplied quality report. It is a research seed, not an independently reviewed historical reference.

## Import status

App adapter: `public/research-1000-1499.js` plus `public/research-1000-1499-apply.js`, and `public/research-1000-1499-tech.js` for the technology explorer.

- 193 society/polity/cultural source rows (51 carried forward, 10 legacy crosswalks, 132 new in the source pack). Existing atlas IDs and chronology remain authoritative; imported observations never replace existing entries. Broad Australian cultural material stays in the archive rather than becoming a single all-history dated civilisation.
- 108 sourced events, with original participant IDs, source IDs, confidence and start/end-exclusive periods kept. Unlinked and multi-society events remain browsable in Compare → Events.
- 39 technology topics and 71 development observations, with source statuses such as `widespread_use` preserved. A dot on a technology graph represents a midpoint of its dated evidence interval, **not an invention year**.
- Other categories retained in `atlas-research-pack.json`: 54 figures, 28 places, 47 relationships, 6 historical maps, 19 geographical-knowledge records, 30 cultural practices, 20 connections, 723 source claims, 95 sources and 22 research gaps.

### Limits and cautions

The original source pack's 58 older source pointers were not reopened and 22 research gaps remain. LOW/DISPUTED claims require review. Dates at 1000 or 1500 may mark an extracted research window, not a society's historical beginning/end. Modern reference-site coordinates do not represent historical political borders. Map-linked resources are citations only, not permission to reproduce the map image.
